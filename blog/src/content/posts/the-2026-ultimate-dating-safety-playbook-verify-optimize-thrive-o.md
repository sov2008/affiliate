## Profile Optimization – The 4-Photo + 3-Sentence Blueprint

### The Science Behind the Numbers (or, Why Your Profile Shouldn't Be a Mess)

- **4-photo rule**: 2 "social" shots, 1 "interest" shot, 1 "clear headshot". This mix signals approachability, lifestyle depth, and authenticity.
- **3-sentence bio**: 1 hook, 1 value proposition, 1 call-to-action (CTA). Keeps attention within the 7-second swipe window.

### Actionable Blueprint (or, How to Write a Decent Bio)

| Section | Content | Tips |
|---------|---------|------|
| **Headline Hook** | "Adventure-seeker who's mastered the art of Sunday brunch." | Use a vivid verb + niche interest. |
| **Value Proposition** | "I'm a product designer turning ideas into reality, looking for a partner to test new recipes." | Show what you bring to a relationship. |
| **CTA** | "Message me your favorite brunch spot and let's plan a taste-test!" | Prompt a specific reply. |

### Photo Checklist (or, How to Avoid Being a Stock Image)

1. **Clear headshot** – eyes on camera, natural smile.
2. **Social proof** – you at a gathering (no group >3 people).
3. **Passion shot** – playing guitar, cooking, hiking.
4. **Travel/landscape** – shows lifestyle, not a stock image.

## Scam Prevention – The "3-Question Filter"

Scammers often rely on vague answers and rapid escalation. Deploy these three questions early to expose inconsistencies:

1. "What's the name of the café you mentioned?"
2. "Can you share a photo of you holding today's newspaper?"
3. "What's a hobby you've never tried but want to?"

**If any answer feels rehearsed or they refuse, block and report.**

## Relationship Psychology – Building Trust Fast

### Progressive Disclosure (or, How Not to Scare Your Date)

- **Phase 1 (Days 1-3)**: Share light, non-personal details (favorite movie).
- **Phase 2 (Days 4-7)**: Introduce values and goals (career aspirations).
- **Phase 3 (Day 8+)**: Discuss past relationships and expectations.

This pacing mirrors natural intimacy curves, reducing "relationship anxiety" and increasing perceived compatibility.

### Mirroring Language (or, How to Not Come Across as a Robot)

Match the partner's communication style (emoji use, sentence length). Studies show mirroring increases perceived similarity by **41%**, boosting match longevity.

### First-Date Timing (or, How Not to Get Stuck in a Never-Ending Conversation)

- **Best window**: 5-7 days after the third meaningful exchange.
- **Why**: Enough rapport to feel safe, but still fresh excitement.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: How often should I re-verify a match?**
A: Perform the Triple-Check within the first 48 hours of meaningful conversation. If the relationship progresses beyond a month, a quick "voice-check" refresh is advisable.

**Q2: Can I trust a profile that's verified on another app?**
A: Not entirely. Verification standards vary. Use FlirtCheck's cross-platform verification tool to confirm consistency across apps.

**Q3: What's the biggest red flag in a first message?**
A: Immediate requests for money, personal finance details, or "quick-move" proposals (e.g., "Let's skip chat, meet tonight").

**Q4: How do I protect my personal data while dating online?**
A: Keep your primary email separate, use a dedicated "dating" phone number (Google Voice or similar), and never share full legal names or ID documents until you've completed in-app verification.
