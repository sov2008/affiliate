## 3. Red Flags & Bot Behaviour (Syslog‑Level Alerts)

| Symptom | What it looks like | Why it matters |
|---------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Over‑the‑top compliments** | “You’re the most beautiful person I’ve ever seen, even before I saw your profile picture.” | Noise that masks lack of substance – akin to a flood of ICMP echo requests. |
| **Dodging personal details** | Vague answers about work, hometown, family. | Missing fields in a config file usually indicate a mis‑configured service. |
| **Story drift** | Timeline or job description shifts between messages. | Inconsistent state – a classic sign of a session that has been reset. |
| **Fast‑track to private apps** | Pushes to WhatsApp/Telegram within hours. | Attempts to bypass the platform’s firewall. |
| **Stilted grammar** | Repeated article errors, unnatural phrasing. | Low‑entropy language model output – think of a badly tuned random number generator. |
| **Instant replies regardless of timezone** | Replies arrive the second a message is sent, even at 3 am GMT. | Bot‑level latency of 0 ms, impossible for a human client. |
| **Copy‑pasted openers** | Identical first lines across multiple matches. | Signature of a scripted payload. |
| **Emoji spam without context** | Random 😂 or 🙈 tossed in. | Placeholder data, like a default MAC address. |

When any of these flags raise a warning level, pause the session and run the radar scan.

## 4. Secure Communication & In‑Person Handshake Playbook  

1. **Dedicated dating email** – Create a throwaway address for initial contact; keep your work mailbox out of the DMZ.  
2. **Data minimisation** – No home address, bank details, or SSN until you have a verified, public‑face‑to‑face interaction spanning several weeks.  
3. **Pre‑date video call** – A 5‑minute live feed is the equivalent of a TLS handshake – it validates the certificate (the person).  
4. **Public first meeting** – Choose a café, park, or any venue with visible CCTV; think of it as a DMZ with monitoring.  
5. **Outbound notification** – Tell a friend the venue, time, and expected return. Use a check‑in app if you enjoy extra logging.  

**Emergency shutdown:** If the counterpart requests money, becomes aggressive, or insists on a secluded location, terminate the TCP session immediately and block the IP (profile).

## Frequently Asked Questions  

**Q: How effective is a reverse‑image search?**  
A: Roughly 73 % of catfish profiles recycle images from elsewhere, so a quick hash lookup catches most of the low‑hanging fruit. AI‑generated faces, however, require the radar’s deeper analysis.  

**Q: Can an AI bot survive a video‑call test?**  
A: Not with current tech. A bot cannot stream an unscripted, live video of a real human – it will either time‑out or serve a pre‑recorded clip, both of which raise the alert level.  

**Q: I’ve already met someone who turned out to be a catfish. What now?**

1. End the interaction calmly and leave the premises.
