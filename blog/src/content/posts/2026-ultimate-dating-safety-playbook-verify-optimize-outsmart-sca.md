## Verify Like a Pro: The 5-Layer Identity Audit

### Layer 1 – Reverse-Image & Metadata Scan
1. Right-click, "Search Image" on Google, or use the free TinEye extension.
2. Look for duplicate uploads on other platforms.
3. Check EXIF data with exif.tools; a missing camera model or GPS tag can signal a generated image.

Example Chat:
You: "Love your hiking pic! Where was that trail?"
Scammer: "Oh, it's a secret spot, can't share." (Red flag – evasive answer)

### Layer 2 – Social-Graph Cross-Check
- Search the username on LinkedIn, Instagram, and Reddit.
- Real people often have consistent posting patterns.

### Layer 3 – Voice/Video Confirmation
- Suggest a quick 30-second video call with a fun prompt.
- Use FlirtCheck Forensic Archive to generate a one-time video link that expires after 5 minutes – no recordings, just live proof.

### Layer 4 – Phone Number & Email Validation
- Request a WhatsApp or Signal number; verify the carrier prefix matches the claimed location.
- Use Hunter.io to see if the email domain is disposable.

### Layer 5 – Behavioral Consistency Test
- Ask a personalized, time-bound question.
- Bots and catfish often default to generic answers or stall.

## Scam Prevention: The "Red Flag, Pause, Probe" Protocol

### Red Flag Identification
| Red Flag | Typical Scam Trigger |
|----------|----------------------|
| Requests for money | Financial exploitation |
| Overly fast intimacy | Emotional manipulation |
| Vague personal details + polished grammar | AI-generated scripts |
| External links to "investment" or "gift" sites | Phishing attempts |

### Pause – Give Yourself Space
- Rule: Never reply within 5 minutes to a request that feels "urgent."
- Use the "Snooze & Verify" button in FlirtCheck app to log the interaction and set a 24-hour review timer.

### Probe – Tactical Questioning
- Script Example:
  - You: "What's the story behind the charity you mentioned?"
  - Scammer: "It's a private fund, can't discuss." (Fails probe → cut off)

- Follow-up: If they dodge, escalate: "I respect privacy, but I need to feel safe. Can we move to a video call?"

## Relationship Psychology: From Banter to Bonding

### Mirror-Matching Language
- Mirror the tone, emojis, and pacing of your match. This creates subconscious rapport.

### Scarcity & Value Signals
- Limited-time invitations trigger a FOMO response, increasing commitment.

### Micro-Commitments Build Trust
- Ask for small, low-risk actions. Once they comply, they're more likely to agree to larger steps.
