## 1️⃣ Verify Like a Pro: The 5‑Layer Identity Audit  

### H3.1 Layer 1 – Reverse‑Image & Metadata Scan  
1. **Right‑click → “Search Image”** on Google or use the free **TinEye** extension.  
2. Look for **duplicate uploads** on other platforms (e.g., a stock photo on a personal profile).  
3. Check EXIF data with **exif.tools**; a missing camera model or GPS tag can signal a generated image.  

> **Example Chat:**  
> **You:** “Hey Alex, love your hiking pic! Where was that trail? 🌲”  
> **Scammer:** “Oh, it’s a secret spot, can’t share.” *(Red flag – evasive answer)*  

### H3.2 Layer 2 – Social‑Graph Cross‑Check  
- Search the username on **LinkedIn, Instagram, and Reddit**.  
- Real people often have **consistent posting patterns** (e.g., weekly gym updates).  

### H3.3 Layer 3 – Voice/Video Confirmation  
- Suggest a **quick 30‑second video call** with a fun prompt (“Show me your favorite coffee mug”).  
- Use **FlirtCheck Verified Portal** to generate a **one‑time video link** that expires after 5 minutes—no recordings, just live proof.  

### H3.4 Layer 4 – Phone Number & Email Validation  
- Request a **WhatsApp or Signal** number; verify the **carrier prefix** matches the claimed location.  
- Use **Hunter.io** to see if the email domain is disposable (e.g., @mailinator.com).  

### H3.5 Layer 5 – Behavioral Consistency Test  
- Ask a **personalized, time‑bound question** (“What’s the name of the coffee shop you mentioned yesterday?”).  
- Bots and catfish often **default to generic answers** or stall.

## 3️⃣ Scam Prevention: The “Red Flag, Pause, Probe” Protocol  

### H3.1 Red Flag Identification  
| Red Flag | Typical Scam Trigger |
|----------|----------------------|
| Requests for money (even “just a coffee”) | Financial exploitation |
| Overly fast intimacy (“I feel a soul connection”) | Emotional manipulation |
| Vague personal details + polished grammar | AI‑generated scripts |
| External links to “investment” or “gift” sites | Phishing attempts |

### H3.2 Pause – Give Yourself Space  
- **Rule:** Never reply within 5 minutes to a request that feels “urgent.”  
- Use the **“Snooze & Verify”** button in FlirtCheck app to log the interaction and set a 24‑hour review timer.  

### H3.3 Probe – Tactical Questioning  
- **Script Example:**  
  - **You:** “I’m curious—what’s the story behind the charity you mentioned?”  
  - **Scammer:** “It’s a private fund, can’t discuss.” *(Fails probe → cut off)*  

- **Follow‑up:** If they dodge, **escalate**: “I respect privacy, but I need to feel safe. Can we move to a video call?”  

### H3.4 Exit Strategy  
- Politely **“I’m stepping offline for a while, let’s reconnect later.”**  
- Block & report via the platform’s **“Report Suspicious Activity”** feature and forward the chat transcript to **FlirtCheck’s Scam‑Watch** team.

## 4️⃣ Relationship Psychology: From Banter to Bonding  

### H3.1 Mirror‑Matching Language  
- Mirror the **tone, emojis, and pacing** of your match. If they use “😂” and short sentences, respond similarly. This creates subconscious rapport.  

### H3.2 Scarcity & Value Signals  
- **Limited‑time invitations** (“I’m free this Friday for a rooftop drinks—only have one spot left!”) trigger a **FOMO** response, increasing commitment.  

### H3.3 Micro‑Commitments Build Trust  
- Ask for **small, low‑risk actions** (“Can you send a photo of your favorite book cover?”). Once they comply, they’re more likely to agree to larger steps (e.g., meeting in person).  

### H3.4 Authentic Humor vs AI‑Generated Gimmicks  
| Metric (2026) | Human‑crafted humor | AI‑generated openers |
|---------------|--------------------|----------------------|
| Response Rate | **62 %** (±4) | 38 % (±6) |
| Long‑term engagement (>3 messages) | 48 % | 22 % |
| Reported as “spam” | 2 % | 15 % |

**Takeaway:** A witty, personal joke (e.g., referencing a detail from their bio) outperforms any generic AI line.
