## 3. Scam Prevention: Spotting the Subtle Tactics  

### 3.1 The “Emergency Money” Script  

**Typical pattern:** “I’m stuck at the airport, my wallet got stolen, can you lend me $200?”  

**Counter‑move:**  
- **Ask for proof** (photo of boarding pass, location tag).  
- **Offer a safe alternative** (e.g., “I can send you a prepaid card, but I need a verified ID first”).  

### 3.2 Deep‑Fake Video Deception  

With AI‑generated videos, look for:  

- **Mismatched lip sync** with audio.  
- **Unnatural eye movement** or lighting inconsistencies.  

**Tool tip:** Use free browser extensions that flag AI‑generated media (e.g., DeepDetect).  

### 3.3 “Too‑Good‑to‑Be‑True” Compliments  

Scammers often flood you with excessive flattery to lower defenses.  

> **Chat Example – Red Flag**  
> **Scammer:** “You’re the most beautiful person I’ve ever seen—like, literally the love of my life already.”  

**Response:** “Thanks! I appreciate the kindness. Let’s keep getting to know each other before we jump ahead.”

## 4. Relationship Psychology: Building Resilience While You Swipe  

### 4.1 Attachment‑Style Awareness  

| Style | Typical Behavior | Safety Tip |
|-------|------------------|------------|
| Secure | Open, balanced communication | Trust but verify |
| Anxious | Seeks constant reassurance | Set clear response windows |
| Avoidant | Keeps emotional distance | Respect boundaries, don’t chase |

### 4.2 Persuasion Tactics to Recognize  

- **Reciprocity trap:** “I’ve sent you a gift, now you owe me a date.”  
- **Scarcity pressure:** “I’m only on the app for a week—let’s meet now.”  

**Counter‑strategy:** Pause, breathe, and evaluate the request against your checklist.  

### 4.3 Emotional Self‑Audit  

After each conversation, ask yourself:  

1. **Did I feel pressured?**  
2. **Did I share any personal data?**  
3. **Did the person respect my verification request?**  

If the answer is “yes” to any, consider ending the interaction.
