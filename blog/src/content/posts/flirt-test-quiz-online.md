## The 4 Primary Digital Flirting Archetypes

Based on interpersonal psychology research, digital flirting habits fall into four primary behavioral quadrants:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 THE FLIRTING ARCHETYPE MATRIX               │
├──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. The Playful Tease         │ 2. The Direct Escalator      │
│ • High banter & wit          │ • Clear intentions & bold    │
│ • Uses irony and tension     │ • Moves to plans swiftly     │
├──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. The Empathetic Connector  │ 4. The Guarded Observer      │
│ • Deep questions & warmth    │ • Waits for other to lead    │
│ • Strong emotional rapport   │ • Highly analytical & cautious│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **The Playful Tease (High Banter / High Tension):** Masters of wit and push-pull dynamics. Strengths: Generates immediate dopamine and intrigue. Pitfall: Can stay in sarcastic mode too long, delaying genuine emotional vulnerability.
2. **The Direct Escalator (High Action / High Confidence):** Focuses on swift progression from match to in-person connection. Strengths: High date conversion rate. Pitfall: Can come across as abrupt if basic rapport is skipped.
3. **The Empathetic Connector (High Warmth / Deep Rapport):** Excels at deep questions and meaningful discussions. Strengths: Builds deep emotional security. Pitfall: Vulnerable to ending up in the dreaded "friend zone" if romantic tension is neglected.
4. **The Guarded Observer (Low Risk / High Caution):** Prefers reacting rather than initiating. Strengths: Avoids awkward mistakes. Pitfall: Conversations frequently stall due to lack of forward momentum.
