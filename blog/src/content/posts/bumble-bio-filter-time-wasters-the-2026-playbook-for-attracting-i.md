## Tactical Section 1: Craft the Core Narrative  

### 🎯 Goal‑First Headline  
Your first line is the headline of your personal ad. Make it **purpose‑centric**, not just flattering.

**Example:**  
> “Engineer building sustainable tech, looking for a partner to co‑author our next adventure – no games, just real connection.”

**Why it works:**  
- **Engineer** signals stability and ambition.  
- **Sustainable tech** adds a niche interest that filters out generic “just looking for fun.”  
- **Co‑author** implies partnership, not a fling.  

### 🧩 Structured Bio Blueprint (150‑200 characters)

| Segment | What to Include | Sample |
|---------|----------------|--------|
| **Who You Are** | Role + unique trait | “Product designer + amateur chef” |
| **What You Want** | Relationship goal | “Seeking a long‑term partnership” |
| **Values** | Core principle | “Honesty > endless texting” |
| **Call‑to‑Action** | Mini‑screening prompt | “If you’re into weekend hikes, drop your favorite trail.” |

**Full Bio Example (180 chars):**  
> “Product designer + amateur chef. Looking for a long‑term partnership built on honesty > endless texting. Weekend hikes are my jam—share your favorite trail.”

## Tactical Section 2: Deploy a Low‑Friction Screening Prompt  

A **mini‑quiz** forces casual daters to reveal their seriousness. Keep it simple, fun, and impossible to ignore.

### 📋 Prompt Templates  

1. **Interest‑Match Prompt** – “What’s one book that changed your outlook? (No spoilers, please!)”  
2. **Value‑Check Prompt** – “Rate honesty on a 1‑10 scale and explain why.”  
3. **Future‑Vision Prompt** – “If we could travel anywhere next month, where would we go?”

### Dialogue Example  

**You:** “If we could travel anywhere next month, where would we go?”  
**Time‑Waster:** “Umm… maybe the mall?”  
**Serious Match:** “I’d love a weekend in Kyoto—temple sunrise, sushi, and a quiet garden walk.”  

**Interpretation:** The mall answer signals low investment; the Kyoto answer shows imagination and intent.
