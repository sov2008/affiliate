## The hidden cost of unverified profiles  

Roughly a third of Bumble accounts never bother with photo or identity verification. In practice that means a sizable slice of every swipe ends up on a bot, a casual flirter, or a plain‑old scammer. Think of your bio as a firewall rule‑set: the tighter the filter, the fewer stray packets reach your inbox.

---

## Key take‑aways  

- **Signal intent early** – phrasing that reads “serious dating” rather than “just having fun”.  
- **Add quiet social proof** – verified badges, niche hobbies, modest achievements.  
- **Insert a low‑overhead screening prompt** – something that forces the indifferent to self‑eliminate.  
- **Reference verification** – a one‑liner about FlirtCheck or a relevant community boosts trust instantly.

### 1. Craft the core narrative  

#### Goal‑first headline  

Your opening line is the SYN packet of a personal ad. It should carry the right flags, not just a payload of vanity.

> “Engineer building sustainable tech, looking for a partner to co‑author our next adventure – no games, just real connection.”

*Why it works*  
- **Engineer** – a stable service class.  
- **Sustainable tech** – a niche port that filters out generic traffic.  
- **Co‑author** – indicates a persistent session, not a one‑off request.

#### Structured bio blueprint (≈150 – 200 characters)

| Segment | What to include | Sample |
|--------|----------------|--------|
| **Who you are** | Role + quirky trait | “Product designer + amateur chef” |
| **What you want** | Relationship goal | “Seeking a long‑term partnership” |
| **Values** | Core principle | “Honesty > endless texting” |
| **Call‑to‑action** | Mini‑screening prompt | “If you’re into weekend hikes, drop your favourite trail.” |

**Full bio example (180 chars)**  
> “Product designer + amateur chef. Seeking a long‑term partnership built on honesty > endless texting. Weekend hikes are my jam—share your favourite trail.”

### 2. Deploy a low‑friction screening prompt  

A tiny quiz works like a TTL‑limited ping: it expires quickly for the uninterested, but the committed respond with full payload.

#### Prompt templates  

1. **Interest‑match** – “What’s one book that changed your outlook? (No spoilers, please.)”  
2. **Value‑check** – “Rate honesty on a 1‑10 scale and explain why.”  
3. **Future‑vision** – “If we could travel anywhere next month, where would we go?”

#### Dialogue snippet  

| You | Match |
|-----|-------|
| “If we could travel anywhere next month, where would we go?” | “Umm… maybe the mall?” |
| “If we could travel anywhere next month, where would we go?” | “I’d love a weekend in Kyoto – temple sunrise, sushi, quiet garden walk.” |

The first reply times out; the second keeps the connection alive.

### 3. Social proof & verification integration  

#### Quiet credibility  

- **Badge mention** – “Verified on FlirtCheck – see my profile for proof.”

#### Seamless insertion

Verification raises the trust score; community involvement shows you’re not just another default route.

### 4. Optimise for Bumble’s algorithm  

- **Keyword placement** – sprinkle terms like *serious dating*, *high‑value profile*, *intentional partner* naturally; stay under a 2 % density to avoid sounding like autogenerated traffic.  
- **Activity cadence** – refresh the bio every 30 days; rotate the screening prompt weekly. Bumble’s ranking engine favours recent packets, so keep the flow alive.
