## 1️⃣ Verify & Vet: The 3‑Step Safety Protocol  

### 1.1. Visual Confirmation  
- **Reverse‑image search** every profile picture (Google Images, TinEye).  
- Look for **consistent EXIF data** (date, location) when possible.  

### 1.2. Cross‑Platform Consistency  
- Check the **username, photo, and bio** across at least two dating platforms (e.g., Tinder & Bumble).  
- Authentic users maintain a **stable digital fingerprint**; sudden changes often signal a fabricated persona.  

### 1.3. Real‑Time Verification  
- **FlirtCheck Verified Portal** offers a free, AI‑backed verification badge after a short video call.  
- **Ask for a live selfie** with a random object (e.g., a coffee cup) and a timestamp—this defeats deep‑fake attempts.

## 3️⃣ Scam Prevention: Spot, Respond, and Protect  

### 3.1. Red‑Flag Checklist  
- **Urgent money requests** (“I’m stuck abroad, can you help?”).  
- **Vague or contradictory personal details** (different ages, jobs).  
- **Overly rapid intimacy** (“We’re soulmates, let’s meet tomorrow”).  
- **Requests to move off‑platform** before a basic video chat.  

### 3.2. The “S.C.A.M.” Response Script  

| Situation | Script | Outcome |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| **Financial ask** | “I’m not comfortable sending money online. If you need help, consider contacting your bank or a local charity.” | Sets a firm boundary without aggression. |
| **Identity doubt** | “I noticed a few inconsistencies in your story—mind clarifying? I value honesty.” | Forces the scammer to reveal truth or quit. |
| **Pressure to meet** | “I love spontaneity, but I prefer a video call first for safety. Let’s schedule a 5‑minute chat?” | Shifts the interaction to a verifiable medium. |

If the counterpart **evades** or **escalates**, **block and report** via the app and optionally submit evidence to **FlirtCheck’s Scam Reporting Hub**.

## 4️⃣ Relationship Psychology: From Chat to Real‑Life Date in 5 Texts  

1. **Establish Common Ground (Text 1)**  
   - *“I saw you love hiking—what’s your favorite trail in the area?”*  
   - **Why:** Shared interests trigger dopamine, building rapport.  

2. **Show Vulnerability (Text 2)**  
   - *“I’ve been meaning to try a pottery class but always get nervous about being a beginner. Ever felt that?”*  
   - **Why:** Controlled self‑disclosure raises intimacy levels (7‑10% rule).  

3. **Introduce a Low‑Commitment Activity (Text 3)**  
   - *“There’s a weekend coffee‑brew tasting at the downtown market Saturday. Want to check it out together?”*  
   - **Why:** Proposes a **public, time‑bounded** meet that feels safe.  

4. **Confirm Logistics (Text 4)**  
   - *“Great! I’ll grab us a spot at 11 am. Text me when you’re near the entrance?”*  
   - **Why:** Sets clear expectations, reducing anxiety.  

5. **Reinforce Positivity (Text 5 – Post‑Date)**  
   - *“Had a blast today—thanks for the great conversation. Want to plan a hike next weekend?”*  
   - **Why:** Closes the loop with a **positive reinforcement loop**, encouraging future dates.  

> **Pro tip:** Use **“mirroring language”** (repeat key words from their messages) to increase perceived similarity—research shows a **13% higher likelihood of a meet‑up**.
