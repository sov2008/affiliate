## Why "Hey" Fails on Bumble: The Psychology of Reply Friction

When you send a generic greeting like *"Hey"*, you inadvertently offload 100% of the cognitive effort onto the other person:

```
Sender: "Hey" ────────► Recipient Mindset:
                         • "Now I have to think of what to say."
                         • "They put zero thought into my profile."
                         • "I'll reply later when I'm less busy."
                                    │
                                    ▼
                         [ Match Expires in 24h ]
```

An exceptional first message reduces reply friction to nearly zero: it gives the recipient an immediate, fun, and effortless hook to bounce back.

## 5 Tested Bumble First Message Templates

### Template 1: The Playful First-Impression Read
* **Opener:** *"You give off strong 'has a curated Spotify playlist for every single mood' energy. Am I spot on or way off?"*
* **Why it works:** Everyone loves discussing their musical taste; it flatters their aesthetic sense while inviting a prompt reaction.

### Template 2: The Two-Choice Dilemma
* **Opener:** *"Crucial Sunday morning test: iced coffee and an impulsive bookstore crawl, or sleeping until noon and ordering takeout?"*
* **Why it works:** Takes 3 seconds to answer, reveals lifestyle alignment, and naturally pre-frames future date ideas.

### Template 3: The Photo Micro-Hook
* **Opener:** *"I need the backstory on that second photo—did you plan that or was it total chaos behind the scenes?"*
* **Why it works:** Proves you looked beyond their primary photo and encourages engaging storytelling.

### Template 4: The Bold Culinary Take
* **Opener:** *"Tell me your most controversial food opinion so I can decide if we can be seen in public together."*
* **Why it works:** Playfully adversarial. People love defending unconventional opinions (e.g., pineapple on pizza, iced coffee in winter).

### Template 5: The Opening Move Reply
* **Prompt:** *"What's your go-to karaoke song?"*
* **Opener:** *"If you say anything other than 2000s pop punk, I might have to revoke your microphone privileges. What's the real answer?"*
* **Why it works:** Directly leverages Bumble's Opening Move feature with charming teasing.

## What to Do When He/She Replies: The 2nd Message Pivot

Receiving an initial response is only half the battle. To keep the match from fizzling out:
1. **Never Revert to Small Talk:** If they answer your food question, don't follow up with *"Cool, what do you do for work?"* Stay inside the banter frame for at least two exchanges.
2. **Match Conversational Energy:** If they send a playful two-line response, reply with a punchy, humorous response of similar length.
3. **Escalate within 48 Hours:** Once mutual humor is established, propose a low-pressure real-world meetup before app fatigue sets in.
