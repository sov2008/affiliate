## 1⃣ Verify & Filter: The 3‑Point Trust Test

| Step | Action | Example in Conversation |
|------|--------|--------------------------|
| **1️⃣ Photo Match** | Run a reverse‑image lookup (Google Lens, TinEye) on the primary picture. | *Match sends a beach snap.* “Nice spot – my reverse‑image search shows the same view on a travel blog. Glad the real traveller is here.” |
| **2️⃣ Social Sync** | Confirm a linked Instagram/Twitter or a LinkedIn profile that passes verification. | *Match drops a LinkedIn URL.* “I see you’re a software engineer at XYZ – good to have the same source of truth on both ends.” |
| **3️⃣ Real‑Time Video** | Request a brief video call before swapping personal numbers. | “You mentioned hiking. How about a 2‑minute video chat so we can verify we’re both not bots?” |

*If any step returns a timeout, drop the connection politely.*

## 4⃣ First‑Date Safety Protocol (FDS‑P)

1. **Public Venue Only** – coffee shop, busy park, or restaurant with staff on hand.  
2. **Share Your ETA** – ping a trusted friend with location and expected return time.  
3. **Transport Independence** – arrange your own ride; never rely on the date’s vehicle.  
4. **Payment Plan** – split the bill or cover it yourself; avoid cash hand‑overs.  
5. **Exit Strategy** – pre‑planned excuse (“morning meeting”) and a discreet “safe word” with a friend.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How can I spot an AI‑generated avatar?**  
A: Inspect physical lighting consistency, ear cartilage symmetry, pupil specular reflections, and hair boundaries. If artifacts or anatomical mismatches appear, verify via a live unscripted video call before proceeding.

**Q: Do verification badges guarantee safety?**  
A: They lower risk dramatically, much like a signed certificate, but they’re not a panacea. Combine the badge with the 3‑point trust test and keep conversations on‑platform until you’re comfortable moving off‑site.

**Q: What if a match pushes to WhatsApp or Telegram immediately?**  
A: State your policy – “I keep chats on the app until I’ve verified we’re both real.” Aggression or threats merit an instant block and report.

**Q: How often should I rotate profile photos?**  
A: Every 3‑4 months, with at least one recent, unedited shot in natural light. Vary hobby images to demonstrate breadth of interest – it signals authenticity and keeps the algorithm’s cache fresh.
