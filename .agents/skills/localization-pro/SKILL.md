# Professional Localization & Transcreation Skill (`localization-pro`)

## 1. Mission & Philosophy
This skill provides professional-grade translation, transcreation (cultural adaptation), and market localization for `promoradar.store` and related multi-market portals.

**Core Principles:**
1. **Transcreation over Literal Translation:** Never translate word-for-word. Adapt idioms, shopping terminology, currency customs, and tone of voice to sound like a native in-market shopper.
2. **Direct-Response Conversion Voice:** Ensure calls to action (CTAs), urgency triggers, and discount statements preserve high click-through intent.
3. **Zero Machine-Translation Artifacts:** Eliminate awkward syntax, mechanical phrasing, and unidiomatic word choices.
4. **Tone Nuance per Target Market:**
   - **US (United States):** Direct, punchy, benefit-first, value-driven (*"Get 50% Off Today"*, *"Clip Coupon"*).
   - **UK (United Kingdom):** Crisp, understated, polite yet compelling (*"Save up to 50% on your order"*, *"Voucher Code"*).
   - **GCC / UAE:** Premium, respectful, hospitality and luxury-oriented (*"Exclusive Privileges"*, *"Complimentary Booking Benefit"*).
   - **EU (DE / FR / ES / IT):** Structured, transparent, precise terms without exaggerated hype.

## 3. Localization Quality Checklist
Before outputting localized content:
- [ ] Are dates formatted according to the locale (`MM/DD/YYYY` for US, `DD/MM/YYYY` for UK/EU)?
- [ ] Are currency symbols positioned correctly (`$50` in US, `50 €` or `€50` in EU)?
- [ ] Are shopping terms natural for that country (e.g. *cart* vs *basket*, *shipping* vs *delivery*)?
- [ ] Does the copy strictly avoid raw affiliate network jargon (`Default Payout`, `CPA terms`, etc.)?
- [ ] Is the emotional punch and benefit preservation 100% intact?
