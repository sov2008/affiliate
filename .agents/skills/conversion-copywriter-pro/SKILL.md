# Elite Conversion Copywriter Skill (`conversion-copywriter-pro`)

## 1. Core Mandate & Quality Gate
Every deal card, title, headline, and CTA must pass the **Bespoke Humanized Conversion Copy Standard** defined in the project constitution (`AGENTS.md`).

### Forbidden Phrases (Instant Disqualification):
- Raw affiliate network jargon: `The promo code is valid...`, `The discount applies to...`, `Exclusive CJ Affiliate discount...`, `Default US/CA terms...`, `Base 4% payout`.
- AI Clichés: `unlock savings`, `unleash your potential`, `elevate your shopping`, `game-changer`, `in today's fast-paced world`, `it's not X, it's Y`, `dive into`.

---

## 2. High-Converting Card Anatomy

### A. Title (Max 65 characters):
- Formula: `[Benefit/Discount] + [Product/Service Key] + [Brand/Niche]`
- Good: `81% Off 2-Year Lightway Privacy Shield + 4 Months Free`
- Good: `Flat $20 Off Sitewide Wholesale Order at Alibaba`
- Bad: `Alibaba Promo Code and Terms 2026`

### B. Subtitle / Description (1–2 concise sentences, max 140 chars):
- Focus on buyer value: what they get, why it's genuine, and what problem it solves.
- Good: `Deduct $20 immediately on electronics, machinery, and bulk supplies. Verified at checkout.`
- Good: `Ultra-fast Lightway VPN protocol across 8 devices with a 30-day money-back guarantee.`
- Bad: `Save money when shopping with this coupon valid until the end of the year.`

### C. Call to Action (CTA):
- Dynamic, action-oriented:
  - Promo code: `"Copy Code"` + masked preview `[ ···T50 ]`
  - Direct deal: `"Get Deal"` / `"Claim 50% Off"` + directional arrow `↗`

---

## 3. Angle Generator Matrix
When writing copy for deals, alternate across 4 proven psychological angles:
1. **Urgency & Scarcity:** *"Ends this weekend"*, *"Limited voucher allotment"*.
2. **Exclusivity / VIP Rate:** *"Official merchant checkout partner"*, *"Exclusive direct cut"*.
3. **Problem-Solution:** *"Protect all family devices in 1 click"*, *"Avoid expensive flight roaming charges"*.
4. **Friction Reduction:** *"Instant checkout deduction"*, *"Zero sign-up fee"*, *"Money-back guarantee"*.
